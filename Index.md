Luxe Bot — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Luxe is a multifunctional Discord bot providing moderation, security, logging, welcome messages, tickets, economy, OwO tracking, giveaways, music, temporary voice channels, and AI assistance. This policy explains how Luxe may collect, use, and store data.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features enabled in a server, Luxe may process or store:

* Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs.
* Server configurations and feature settings.
* Economy balances, inventory, rewards, and OwO statistics.
* Moderation records, warnings, and security logs.
* Ticket settings and information needed to operate tickets.
* Message content temporarily processed for commands, anti-spam, link filtering, OwO tracking, and AI replies.

Luxe does not intentionally collect passwords or Discord account login credentials.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used to operate the bot, provide requested features, enforce server rules, prevent spam and abuse, maintain leaderboards, and troubleshoot technical problems.

4. Storage and Security

Some bot data may be stored in a database outside Discord, including SQLite. Data retention depends on the feature and configuration. Reasonable measures should be taken to prevent unauthorized access, but no storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. AI and Third-Party Services

When users request AI assistance, relevant message text may be sent to the configured AI provider to generate a response. Music and other integrated features may also rely on third-party services. Those services process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Sharing

Luxe does not intend to sell users’ personal information. Data may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate requested features or disclosed when legally required.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Some information is retained while needed to provide bot features, maintain server settings, or preserve economy and moderation records. Users or authorized server administrators may request deletion of eligible data from the bot owner. Certain records may need to be retained for legitimate security or operational reasons.

8. User Choices

Server administrators can disable optional bot features where supported. Users can contact the bot owner to ask what information is held about them or request deletion of eligible stored data.

9. Children’s Privacy

Luxe is intended for use in accordance with Discord’s minimum age requirements and applicable laws. The bot is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as Luxe’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at the same public URL.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact the Luxe bot owner through the official Luxe/Fuegos Discord support server.

⸻

By using Luxe, server administrators and users should be aware that information may be processed as described in this policy.
